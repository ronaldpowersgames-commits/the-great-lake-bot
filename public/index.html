🌊 How to use The Lake

      
    

    

      
🌊 The Basics

      
💬
Type anything in the box at the bottom. The Lake reflects back what it sees.

      
📄
Drop a Chat-Log — attach transcripts, meeting notes, screenshots, or chat exports.

      
📷
Take a Photo — snap a whiteboard, document, or screenshot directly into chat.

      
📎
Multiple attachments — attach several files at once. All will be read and analyzed together.

      
🎤
Voice input — tap the mic button and speak your thoughts directly into The Lake.

    

    

    

      
🧠 Reading People & Dynamics

      
💬
Paste a chat thread — The Lake decodes tone, intent, power plays, and what's really being said.

      
🖼️
Drop a screenshot — text message, email, Slack thread. The Lake reads the behavior.

      
🎙️
Paste a transcript — meeting notes, call recordings. The Lake surfaces what the data is really saying.

      
🔥
Something feels off? Describe the situation. The Lake maps hidden dynamics.

      
🎯
Before a hard conversation — drop the context and ask what to watch for.

    

    

    

      
☀️ A daily dip

      
⚔️
Debate Me is in the fifth homepage pair with Mirror Mode. Bring a position or ask the Lake to pick a topic. Expect a spirited, fair challenge to your reasoning. Tap End debate for a brief recap and one way to sharpen your argument.

      
🪞
Mirror Mode lets you experience a simulation of talking with yourself. Open Mirror in the dock, choose Pure Mirror or Mirror Lab, and confirm your own communication examples. You play the other person. Inspect evidence to pause, Resume to continue, or Break the Mirror for a debrief and one practical adjustment. Generated dialogue stays labeled as a simulation and is excluded from personal learning. Blind comparison within an active Mirror lets you compare a generated response with a real reply kept out of the generation request, then reveal which is which.

      
📅
Start the day with Today on the Lake: name one thing that matters or feels stuck. Pick one next move you can make in five minutes.

      
💦
Splash gives you a surprising fact. Riptide asks a curious question. Ride a Wave helps you explore whatever’s on your mind, one question at a time.

      
🎤
Type or speak a few words. Try “What am I assuming?” or “What did I notice about myself today?” Come back for a quick check-in, or use Patterns Across Chats to look for recurring themes.

      
📄
Transcripts: A readable, text-based PDF works best when you want to keep the original layout and page references. Meeting Currents searches saved text-based PDFs, TXT, and Transcript Splitter ZIP parts. Scans need searchable text. Tap tSummarize for the essential takeaway in a couple of sentences.

    

    

    

      
🏊 Types

      
↔️
Swipe the homepage cards sideways to explore conversation Types, then scroll within a Type to reveal its second set of prompts.

      
🎯
Social reads people and relationships. Still Waters supports reflection. Internal sharpens decisions and self-knowledge. Podcast Style draws out stories. Creative and specialist Types add writing, music, drawing, leadership, and other focused starters.

    

    

    

      
🔗 Sharing & privacy

      
🌊
Invite someone shares the public Lake homepage so they can start their own Tide.

      
🔐
Share this Tide creates a read-only link for this conversation. Add an optional passphrase when the contents need another layer of privacy.

      
↗️
A message’s Share action opens a simple preview where you can share, copy, or export that Wave as text.

      
🧰
Tools keeps advanced utilities out of the main chat. Transcript Splitter lives there, along with an aspirational Post Imagery prototype retained for future Studio work.

🎭 Lake Moods

      
Choose 🎭 Lake Mood in the header or Settings. Switch message by message with a command like /drip What should I try first? Your choice stays active until you change it.

      
💧
Drip — one word to three sentences. Best for a quick answer.

      
💦
Shallow — short splashes. Best for a concise but complete read.

      
🌊
Calm — reflective, deep, gentle. Best for processing emotions.

      
🔍
Deep Dive — structured, precise, analytical. Best for transcripts and decision-making.

      
⛈️
Stormy — punchier drafts, playful bite and candid challenges. Same conversation, sharper voice.
